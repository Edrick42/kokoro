# Moluun — anatomy references

Real-animal references following Whitlatch's Step 0 (gather animal anatomy
before designing). All sources are Wikimedia Commons; please verify the
specific license on each file's Commons page before any commercial use of a
crop. The originals + their licenses live at the URLs below.

## Why these animals

The Moluun is a **bipedal-capable forest mammal**. Its closest real-world
analogues by trait:

- **Red panda (*Ailurus fulgens*)** — palette (warm reddish-brown), bushy
  ringed tail, ear shape (triangular tufted), bipedal stance when alarmed
  or feeding, climbing-friendly anatomy with semi-retractile claws.
- **Common wombat (*Vombatus ursinus*)** — body mass (round, compact,
  low center of gravity), short stubby legs, juvenile kindchenschema (head
  ~55-60% of body height), small dark eyes set low.
- **Koala (*Phascolarctos cinereus*)** — large rounded fluffy ears, soft
  silver-gray fur texture, joey clinging pose, tucked sleeping posture.
